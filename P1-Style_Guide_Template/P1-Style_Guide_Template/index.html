What our company does and offers

Our company is a fictional independant game developement studio. The products it lists are digital video games for sale, with descriptions of their gameplay and features.

Who our company is to potential customers, as a person.

Our company, to potential customers, can be seen as game designers, programmers, and various artists for all aspects of gampelay, including sound effects, music, visual assets, character designs, and much more. The company embodies all these people as the unified face of video game projects, and the creators of the customers favourite games.

Why might our company entice someone to purchase a product or service

Our company will entice gamers to play its games in order to generate revenue and share their artistic creations. The company may promote their games to gamers with an interest to similar video game genres or playstyles.
