Company

What our company does and offers

Our company is a fictional independant game developement studio. The products it lists are digital video games for sale, with descriptions of their gameplay and features.

Who our company is to potential customers, as a person.

Our company, to potential customers, can be seen as game designers, programmers, and various artists for all aspects of gampelay, including sound effects, music, visual assets, character designs, and much more. The company embodies all these people as the unified face of video game projects, and the creators of the customers favourite games.

Why might our company entice someone to purchase a product or service

Our company will entice gamers to play its games in order to generate revenue and share their artistic creations. The company may promote their games to gamers with an interest to similar video game genres or playstyles, outlined in the company personae.

Customer Personae

Supportive Sally

Our first persona is "Supportive Sally", who is interested in supporting local, independant game studios. Sally is a young adult and an avid gamer and represents a demographic that is used to playing various video games, but takes an interest in smaller, local creators like ourselves.

Casual Carl

Our second persona is "Casual Carl" who, like Sally, is a young adult, but is less of a regular gamer. Carl semi-frequently plays games on his computer as a pastime. Unlike Sally, he doesn't care about who made a game, Carl simply looks for games that are more relaxed with low-stakes, such as our Bird Game franchise.

Branding

Fonts

Primary font: Clash Display

Legible, fun. clear, big, and playful. but still forword leaning.

Works as a loud voice of the brand for headlines, logo, section headings and callouts It reads confidently at large sizes without losing personality, which fits a games brands that wants to feel alive other the edgy corporte feel.

Secondary font: Satoshi

Calmer and highly legible.

Used for parts of ther site where analyse the product desciptions, summaries, checkout and footer text. Keeps the site welcoming, trustworthy and easy to browse. Not overwhelming the users

Font Pairings

Clash Display is able to have fun and be bold, while Satoshi sits back and explains. Providing the balance we look for in the website we achieve to build.

Colour Palettes

  • Bright Blue: #5C8DF6

    • Primary Brand Colour. Bold but still playful.

  • Cobalt: #2036A8

    • Still blue but not black. It acts as a good contrast against brighter colours.

  • Bright Orange: #FF7A00

    • Bright, high engery accent colours for hightlihgts and CTAs against the blues

  • Sunny Yellow: #FFD21F

    • Used a secondary accent colours.

  • Cream: #FBF5E6

    • Base background. Off-white so the bright colours sit nicely instead on top of a harsh white

Imagery & Art Direction

Something edgy and forward like a games brand, but never boring, blunt, or bland. Imagery is bold and saturated, with dynamic compositions and playful details that keep it fun, pop, and confident while still selling the product.
