Bird Game 3: Final Feathers

A grand finale to a high-flying trilogy.

The epic conclusion to the Bird Game series! Soar as your favourite bird one last time to put an end to the evil forces! Also featuring a new Arcade Mode with loads of casual fun for the perfect pastime!

About This Game

Bird Game 3 continues Bird's awe-inspiring story in its returning campign mode! Face against all new obstacles, including new Owl bosses, new dangerous forest environments and much more! For lover's of Bird Game's casual gameplay, Bird Game 3 also brings with it revamped singleplayer freeplay experience with returning sandbox and flight simulator , so you can live your bird life in incredible detail.

Features

20+ Hour Campign Content
New Owl Bosses and Cat Enemies
Revamped Flight Simulation/Physics
Sandbox Mode with 30+ Environments and 60+ Obstacles
