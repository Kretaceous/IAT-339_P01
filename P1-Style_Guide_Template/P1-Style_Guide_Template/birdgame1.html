About This Game

Bird Game is a game about birds! Play as a species of your choice in a revolutionary flight simulation/sandbox game and freely live your day-by-day life, like a bird would. Multiple different modes provide different types of gameplay, such as Bird Life for a casual experience with up to 2 players, or a more competitive 1v1 mode with plenty of strategy.
