Bird Game: Wacky Wings

The best game to ever exist.

Take to the skies in the original pioneer of the Bird Game series! Fly your way through enemies, gather sticks to build your nest, and hunt worms in this casual, yet ridiculously fun adventure!

About This Game

Bird Game is a game about birds! Play as a species of your choice in a revolutionary flight simulation/sandbox game and freely live your day-by-day life, like a bird would, Or play through Bird's story in the campaign mode.

Multiple different modes provide different types of gameplay, such as Bird Life for a casual experience with up to 2 players, or a more competitive 1v1 mode with plenty of strategy.

Features

30 Bird Types
100 different nest variations
Couch Co-op
Splitscreen PvP
