About Us

Phoenix Games
Reaching the skies.

We’re a small indie game studio, passionate about creating immersive simulation-type experiences that aim to give players a sense of freedom and fun.

With a deep appreciation for flight simulators and air combat games from our childhood, we're inspired to contribute our own ideas to this space and create the types of fun that we seek as players.

As a result, Phoenix Games was formed in 2018 and has since grown steadily to the studio we are now. With each new title, we continue to refine our unique approach to game design, aiming to deliver the best bird gameplay in the industry.
